## 2. Method

1. Extracted every numeric id and every `NV....._CTRL_CMD_...` name from the 140 inventory rows (111 rows carry an id, 60 a name).
2. Searched every cloned tree with fixed-string, case-insensitive grep: the id as `0x<8 hex>`, as `0x<short>` (leading zeros dropped), as bare 8-hex, and the CMD names (324 patterns in all). Word-boundary filter on the short forms. Hits were assigned to rows by script; noise ids (`0x5000`, `0x10002`, `0x31000`, `0x402c`, `0x640000`) were reviewed by hand.
3. For every hit that defines a control, resolved the params struct and its size: tinygrad via the generated `SIZE` constants; the ogkm 565 fork and the nouveau excerpts by compiling the headers with gcc (`sizeof` of the FINN-named `*_PARAMS` struct). Compared with the observed size.
4. For the `const-*` and `fam-*` rows, searched by topic (PTE kinds, report semaphore, display classes, MC engine indices, GSP boot, fwsec descriptor, caps page, head timing, LUT, DCB types, DVI clock limit and so on) and read the matching source lines.
5. Decoded the three RPC traces of the old tree (`scripts/rpctrace/decode_rpctrace.py --controls`, `ctrl_payload_pairs.py --dump`) to read reply sizes and a few values on GA106, GA102 and AD102.

Verdict semantics (stricter for `reply-values` rows): OBTAINABLE = a public source defines the control id and layout and its size equals the observed one (or a source states the rule). PARTIAL = id, name, bit or field layout exists but not the observed values, or the size does not match, or only our own traces hold it. NOT_FOUND = nothing in any searched source (each NOT_FOUND row lists its patterns). UNKNOWN = none; every row could be decided. **A source that only mirrors ogkm** (tinygrad autogen, the tinygrad ogkm fork) is tagged `[ogkm mirror]` in the notes: its OBTAINABLE adds no information beyond the ogkm check. **FACT_ONLY** marks nova-core (GPL-2.0): take the fact, re-confirm it by our own measurement.
`MEASURED(...)` in the notes means found in the named source text or trace; `INFERRED` is our reading.

## 4. The old tree as a source (provenance)

`/workspace/nvkvm-rs` holds no third-party table. Its entries came from (a) our own RPC traces recorded by a recorder patched into the open driver, on three real boards: GA106 (RTX 3060, open 580.159.04, 2026-08-03), GA102 (RTX 3090) and AD102 (RTX 4090, open 575.51.03); (b) static reading of ogkm-580.159.04 for its classification (`docs/reference/gsp_control_classification.tsv`: 22 bit-15 "GSS-legacy" controls have no header because RM forwards them to the GSP); (c) the capability allowlist derived from gVisor nvproxy. ## 5. The 15 most important findings

1. **Tables covering many versions.** tinygrad's `nv_570/580/610.py` are machine-generated mirrors of three ogkm versions with an explicit `SIZE` for each of about 1100 controls (MIT). They cover the 0080/2080 rows completely (every defined row matched the observed size at some version) and make version drift visible. They carry no display (0073) or 5070 controls. Ref: `tinygrad:868263e:tinygrad/runtime/autogen/nv_580.py` (and the other two). They add nothing the ogkm check does not have.
2. **Layouts drift between driver branches, and the Windows 580.88 reply follows an earlier layout in some cases.** `0x20802a0f` is 24 B at 565/570, 28 B at 580 (ogkm `2af9f1f`) and 32 B at 610, and the observed reply is 24 B; `0x20800a30` is 384/384/512/2048 B (observed 512); `0x20800afe` 8/8/16/16 B (observed 16); `0x00800292` 404/404/804/804 B (observed 804). Which struct a guest sends depends on the branch, so a per-branch row (as `const-hostabi-rows` has) is required.
3. **`0x730122` is not what the inventory observed.** nouveau r535 (535.113.01) names it `SYSTEM_GET_CONNECT_STATE` with a 16 B struct; from 565 and in nouveau r570 the same control is `0x730108`. The observed 8 B echo `{0, displayId}` matches neither. Display system ids were renumbered between 535 and 565 (`GET_SUPPORTED` `0x730120` to `0x730107`, `GET_ACTIVE` `0x730126` to `0x73010c`). Ref: `linux:a5ebb762:drivers/gpu/drm/nouveau/nvkm/subdev/gsp/rm/r535/nvrm/disp.h:187`, `.../r570/nvrm/disp.h:117`.
4. **`MC_ENGINE_IDX_GSP` is 49 at 535.113.01 and 50 at 570.144** (`MC_ENGINE_IDX_DISP` stays 2). The inventory's value 50 is the 570+ one. Ref: `.../r535/nvrm/engine.h:51`, `.../r570/nvrm/engine.h:62`.
5. **Report semaphore: the inventory claim "identical on Hopper" is contradicted, and Blackwell is obtainable.** Mesa's class headers have `SET_REPORT_SEMAPHORE_A..D` at `0x1b00..0x1b0c` on Turing to Blackwell, but `A_OFFSET_UPPER` is 7:0 on TURING_A, AMPERE_A/B and ADA_A and **24:0 on HOPPER_A (`clcb97.h:4039`) and BLACKWELL_A/B (`clcd97.h:4026`, `clce97.h`)**. Mesa also carries full Blackwell headers where ogkm names only the class id. Ref: `mesa:37bf953a58d8:src/nouveau/headers/nvidia/classes/clcb97.h:125,4039` vs `clc997.h:125,4040`.
6. **PTE kind table confirmed by an independent source.** nouveau `tu102_mmu_kind` (`nvkm/subdev/mmu/tu102.c:32-42`) equals kayfabe's uncompressed-kind table entry for entry, and Hopper reuses it (`mmu/gh100.c:16`). Mesa and open-gpu-doc `dev_mmu` headers list the kinds (`SMSKED_MESSAGE` `0x0F` at Turing and Hopper). No `dev_mmu.h` exists for Ada or Blackwell in nouveau or Mesa.
7. **Per-die values differ in our own traces: `GMMU_GET_STATIC_INFO` (`0x20800a59`).** GA106 and GA102 reply `0x31000 / 0x120c20`, AD102 replies `0x54000 / 0x220c20`; `BIF_GET_STATIC_INFO` (`0x20800aac`) differs in its first byte between GA106 and the others. kayfabe advertises the GA106 values for every die (`const-gmmu-static`). MEASURED, own tier-3 data, not third-party.
8. **Constants that are stable across three dies in our traces:** `0x20802a08` fault-method-buffer `0x5000`, `0x20800a3d` FECS record size 128, `0x20809009` `{0, 13}`, `GET_NUM_HEADS` 12 B and `GET_ACTIVE` 16 B. A rule needs a mechanism (strategy 3), so these remain "consistent so far".
9. **Only 1 of 35 "undefined" ids is published anywhere.** `0x208081ea` is `NV2080_CTRL_CMD_RUSD_GET_SUPPORTED_FEATURES` (4 B, matching the observed 4 B) in the ogkm 580 mirror (`nv_580.py:23410`), not in 570 or 610. The 22 bit-15 GSS-legacy controls and the 0073/00809908 unnamed ones have no public header in any source: tier 3 is the only route (our traces give sizes for 10 of them).
10. **Display caps page layout is published.** open-gpu-doc `classes/display/clc773.h` (671 lines, MIT) defines `NVC773_DISP_CAPABILITIES` registers (`SYS_CAP` head/SOR/DSI existence, `SYS_CAPB` and so on), so the 99 captured words can be decoded; the per-die values are hardware reads. `AD102_DISP = 0xC770` is in nouveau `include/nvif/class.h:110`. Head and window counts are read from hardware by nouveau (`gv100_head_cnt`, `gv100_disp_wndw_cnt`), not tabulated.
11. **Bit and field facts for the display constants.** `SET_GET_BLANKING_CTRL` BLANK 0:0 / UNBLANK 1:1 (`clc37d.h:292-298`) match the observed 0x1 / 0x2; `HEAD_SET_MIN_FRAME_IDLE` LEADING 14:0 / TRAILING 30:16 (`clc37d.h:949-951`) match 0x10002 = (2,1); nouveau names bit 0 of `0x611800+head*4` LOADV (`gv100.c:1052-1062`); DCB spec `0x61 = HDMI-A` (`open-gpu-doc/DCB/DCB-4.x-Specification.html:9206`); DVI single link 165000 kHz (`nouveau_connector.c:1070`).
12. **Many-die tables exist, but not the inventory's.** Mesa `sm_for_chipset` (chipset to SM), Mesa `g_nv_name_released.h` (PCI id to name), nova-core chipset/architecture table and boot-firmware choice per architecture (`gsp/hal.rs:61-70`, GPL, FACT_ONLY), nouveau per-chip engine lists (`nvkm/engine/device/base.c:2704-2865`). Useful for family detection and for `fam-*`, not for the per-die constants.
13. **gVisor nvproxy is the public per-version table**: 535.104.05 to 620.51.0 with checksums and a supported/unsupported marker per version (`version.go:156-1307`). Newer than kayfabe's pinned rows (it lists 595.71.05, 610.x, 615.x, 620.x). It states which controls exist per version, not their struct layouts (those come from the mirrors).
14. **Heartbeat mailboxes, per-runlist CHRAM, VEID step size and LTC slices are not in nouveau or nova-core** (0 hits; only the `ltcCount` struct field). Those family answers stay ogkm-derived.
15. **Nothing useful from the NVML family or old tools.** nvidia-container-toolkit, go-nvml/go-nvlib, nvtop, nvml-wrapper, DCGM, nvtrust, gdrcopy and nvidia-modprobe/settings/xconfig carry no RM control table. envytools is pre-Volta (last commit 2023-10-26). vgpu_unlock-rs has RM ids only for vGPU controls. The ogkm versions reachable through third parties for the ogkm agent: 565.57.01, `81fe4fb417c8` (570), `2af9f1f0f7de` (580), 610.43.03; nouveau excerpts 535.113.01 and 570.144.

## 6. Caveats

- Struct sizes from nouveau and the 565 fork were compiled on x86-64 with gcc; RM structs use explicit padding, so equal sizes are expected but not proven for every field.
- The tinygrad struct resolution used naming heuristics for a few KGR/INTERNAL controls (the 565 fork's FINN struct name was tried first); where the struct was not resolved the 565 size is the one reported.
- `matches_observed` compares a struct size with the reply size only; it says nothing about the values.
- The old-tree numbers come from different drivers (575.51.03, 580.159.04) and boards than the Windows 580.88 AD104 capture; the decoder output was read, not re-captured.
- Disk: only the controller worktree `/data/kf-tc-3p` (docs only) was used; clones lived on the host and were removed after the last push.
