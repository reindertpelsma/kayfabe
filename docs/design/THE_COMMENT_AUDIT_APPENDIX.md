## Headline findings (the five that should shape the rewrite plan)

1. **67 % of all doc citations in comments point at archived (SUPERSEDED) docs.** 1 340 `.md` citations: **895 → `docs/archive/`** (banner: "do not cite it as current", archived w822), 269 → live `docs/design/`, 176 → docs that exist only in the C repo or elsewhere. `l1_concurrency.md` alone: 148 cites; `execution_plane_increments.md`: 102. Per-crate archived ratio: trace 12/12, crec 7/7, vmm-qemu 29/32, chips 19/21, rmrpc 40/45, mmu 42/48, abi 101/137, core 87/114, device 93/128, qemu-raw 104/187, isolate-host 81/126. Cleanest: cuda 1/15, linux-raw 24/64.
2. **The scrub contradiction is live in four files.** `fwd/lib.rs:1303` + `qemu-raw/shim.rs:21133` pin "the CeUtils scrub is **forged**, never forwarded — on every plane, under every value of `CE_EXECUTOR_ENV`" (citing archived `l1_concurrency.md §12.26`), while `qemu-raw/shim.rs:21301` (w800, owner 2026-09-19) and `rt/device.rs:341` (w806) say "scrub and CE never executed on CPU, always on GPU". The new design (§7/§8) sides with the latter. `rt/device.rs:9111` still states the owner ruling as `KERNEL → emulate`.
3. **Five defaults are stated both ways inside `kayfabe-qemu-raw/src`** (the class CLAUDE.md names as the tree's most expensive): `KAYFABE_FB_STORE` (`arena` at `shim.rs:16450`, `deviceview.rs:1134` vs `device` at `shim.rs:16245`, `scratchpad.rs:414`, `deviceview.rs:1196`); `KAYFABE_ISOLATES` (`Stillborn` at `shim.rs:20503` vs `Real` at `:20772` — and `Real` "since w760" at `:20772` vs "since w763" at `:21294`); `KAYFABE_VAS_OWNER` (`isolate` at `scratchpad.rs:261`, `shim.rs:16464` vs `k` at `scratchpad.rs:339`); `KAYFABE_SCRATCHPAD` (off at `lib.rs:100`, `scratchpad.rs:52,111`, `shim.rs:15921` vs on at `scratchpad.rs:413`); dirty gate + PT sweep (on at `shim.rs:22055`, `:12184` vs off at `shim.rs:22982-22984`); `CE_EXECUTOR` (`Local` default at `shim.rs:21214` vs `Host` at `:21259`).
4. **ogkm citations are reliable (17/20 exact); doc citations are not (4/4 archived paths, 2/4 misrepresent).** Three ogkm misses are each copy-pasted 3–6×: `ce_utils.c:349` (an assert, not the timeout — ×3 in shim.rs), `rpc.c:11085` "copies **whenever** paramsSize≠0" (drops three guards — ×6 across rmrpc/abi), `kernel_graphics.c:2420` "zero entries" (it is 32).
5. **The lock-rank numbering is stated three different ways in `kayfabe-util`** (`lock.rs:105-110` vs `lock.rs:343-344` vs `lockwitness.rs:37-38`), and the vCPU-blocking rule is stated both as "inline_exceptions must be 0" (`util/trapwitness.rs:62`, `rmladder.rs:9518`) and "passthrough doorbells are inline on the vCPU" (`linux-raw/window_unsafe.rs:434`, `isolate/lib.rs:2913`, owner 2026-09-13). The design sides with the latter.
